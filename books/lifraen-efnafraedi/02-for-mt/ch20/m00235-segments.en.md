Reactions of Carboxylic Acids: An Overview

<!-- SEG:m00235:para:para-00001 -->
We commented earlier in this chapter that carboxylic acids are similar in some respects to both alcohols and ketones. Like alcohols, carboxylic acids can be deprotonated to give anions, which are good nucleophiles in S[[sub:N]]2 reactions. Like ketones, carboxylic acids undergo addition of nucleophiles to the carbonyl group. However, carboxylic acids also undergo other reactions characteristic of neither alcohols nor ketones.
