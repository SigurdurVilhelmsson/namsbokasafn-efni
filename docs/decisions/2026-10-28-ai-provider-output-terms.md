## Question

The AI term pass (CLAUDE.md § Purpose, the second text layer) sends Málstaður MT output to Anthropic's API and keeps
every request and response as a run record under `books/efnafraedi-2e/ai-review/`. This repository is public, so
committing those records publishes the model's outputs. SD10(b) asks what the AI provider's published terms say about
those outputs, and requires that answer on file in the same commit as the first public AI log.

## Decision

The run records and the text revised from them are published on the strength of Anthropic's **Commercial Terms of
Service, effective 2025-06-17**, whose output clauses are quoted verbatim below. Under section B, the customer owns
its Outputs and Anthropic assigns to the customer whatever rights it has in them.

## Reasoning

### What the terms settle

- **Ownership.** Section B gives the outputs to the customer, so the provider's terms put no restriction on
  committing the run records or licensing the revised text with the rest of the book. The licence for that text is
  still set per book in `book-config.json`, and the MT provider's terms are a separate question (SD10(a)).
- **Editor review.** D.3 makes human review the customer's responsibility. That matches the 2026-10-28 ruling that
  AI edits reach editors only as accept/reject suggestions, never as starting text.
- **D.4 binds the customer's use of the Services.** It does not mention Outputs, and publishing records of the
  Services' use is not building a competing product. Whether a third party may train a model on the published corpus
  is not answered by these terms, because that party is not a party to them.
- **Training on project data.** Section B bars Anthropic from training on the inputs, which include Málstaður MT text
  and ruled terminology.

### What was checked and what was not

- **Checked:** the quoted wording is word-for-word the page text with the hash above, found by string search of the
  raw page, not a summary. The only change is typographic: the page's curly quotes and apostrophes are written here
  as straight ones.
- **Not checked:** whether the account the API key belongs to is governed by a separately signed agreement that
  overrides the public Commercial Terms. The register records the key as org-level and not scoped to a workspace;
  if a separate agreement exists, it governs and this record must be superseded.
- **Not legal advice.** This is the project's reading of the provider's published terms, not counsel's review.

## Consequences

- The AI layer's run records (prompts, model, parameters, inputs, outputs, token counts) may be committed to this
  public repository. That is what makes the second text layer reconstructible for the research report (CLAUDE.md
  § Purpose).
- The terms can change. The record pins the 2025-06-17 version by its effective date and a text hash. A later run
  under a newer version needs a new record that supersedes this one, and the version history page is where to check.
- SD10's other questions (the MT provider, SÁM/Íðorðabankinn, the chemistry society, IceG2P) remain open and are
  tracked in the register, not here.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Keep the AI run records off the repository until counsel reviews the terms.** Rejected: CLAUDE.md § Purpose
   requires the AI layer to be kept with its prompts, inputs, outputs and change log, and section B already assigns
   the outputs to the customer. An off-repo copy is what § Purpose calls not durable enough for a publication.
2. **Paraphrase the terms instead of quoting them.** Rejected: a paraphrase cannot be checked against a later
   version of the page, and the point of SD10(b) is a record that can be compared.
