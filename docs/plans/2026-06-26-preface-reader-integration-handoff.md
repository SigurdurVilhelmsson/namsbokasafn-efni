## What's left (vefur side — this handoff)

The rendered `formali.html` exists but **the reader won't show it yet**:

- `static/content/efnafraedi-2e/toc.json` is auto-generated by **`scripts/generate-toc.js`**
  (invoked by `scripts/sync-content.js`). It currently starts at **chapter 1** — there is no
  preface/front-matter entry, and no `preface`/`frontMatter` key in the toc.
- The book metadata already declares the preface: efni `server/data/chemistry-2e.json` and
  `01-source/collection-order.json` both have `"preface": "m68662"`. So the *intent* is known;
  the reader just doesn't surface it.

   `toc.json` as front matter (a dedicated `preface`/`frontMatter` entry, **not** "Chapter 0").
   Use the `"preface"` field already present in the source book data rather than inventing a
   numbered chapter. Slug: `0-1-formali` (file `0-1-formali.html`).
2. **Reader nav / routing** — render a **"Formáli"** entry in the chapter navigation (likely
   before Chapter 1) and ensure the section route serves `0-1-formali.html`. Check the
   `[bookSlug]/kafli/...` routing and the nav/sidebar component.
3. **Sync the content** — `node scripts/sync-content.js --source ../namsbokasafn-efni` pulls the

   the regenerated toc has a clean preface entry (no stray "chapter 0").
4. **Verify in the reader** — the formáli appears in nav, opens, shows Icelandic front matter +
   the English "About the authors" attribution (this English section is intentional).
