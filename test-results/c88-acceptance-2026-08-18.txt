(dedupeAltSegments), not assumed from its docstring: the function splices REMOVED entries
only out of `segments` (line 648, `segments.splice(idx, 1)`); its walk over `structure`

splices, or otherwise removes a `{type:'media'}` structure node. So this census's counts
are NOT touched by dedup at all, in either direction: they reflect what actually sits in
the structure tree regardless of how many (if any) segment-level merges fired. That the
structure total (197) matches the segment-level delta (197) exactly is therefore a genuine,
non-tautological agreement — it additionally means dedup fired ZERO times among the
newly-emitted C88 positions specifically (a merge there would have left the structure count

Recipe (not committed as a script; reproducible from this description): walk

{type:'media'} node); {type:'figure'} is not descended into (pre-existing, different alt
carrier, out of this census's scope).
