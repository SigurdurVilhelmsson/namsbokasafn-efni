Each step is a fixed rule, verified word by word; nothing else differs.

- Ruthenium cases follow BÍN: `rúþeni` (dative) → `rúþeníum`, BÍN's main dative. Other `-ín` names turned `-íum` take the same main dative (`osmíni` → `osmíum`).
- Names whose official form is not in BÍN (mostly superheavies) occur here only in the nominative, in the periodic-table alts, so the form is the official name itself.
- Not changed: derived words already on the official stem (`aktíníðaröðina`, `aktíníðaröðinni`), and `aktiníð` (no accent; a derived word no ruling covers).
