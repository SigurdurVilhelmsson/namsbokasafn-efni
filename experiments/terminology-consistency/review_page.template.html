Taught-term consistency: July MT vs the September re-MT

Chemistry, all 21 chapters. A taught term is an entry in the chapter's own key-terms list. A term counts as consistent when at least 80% of the sentences that use it carry the same Icelandic word as the chapter's key-terms entry.

What to decide

  • The list opens on the terms that lost consistency. Use “Show” to see the rest, including improvements.
  • Per chapter, which MT goes forward. The control sits at the top of each chapter.
  • Where both versions list the same word but the new text strays from it, the choice is whether the text should keep to that word.
  • Per term, which Icelandic word is right: the July word, the new word, or another. A ruling here can become a house-style ruling and reach the next buy.
  • This measures consistency, not correctness. A version that is consistently wrong still scores well, so the word choice is yours.
  • “Key-terms entry ≠ text” is cheap: one short segment disagrees with a chapter text that is fine. “Text drifted” is the expensive kind.
  • Corrected after your first look: the matcher now recognises inflections such as einsleita efnablöndu. The first version wrongly scored them as not using the term, which made the July text look worse than it is. All numbers on this page come from the corrected run.
  • The live site shows the July key terms in every chapter: 269 July-only hits and 0 new-only hits. The control on our new pages found the new words in all 21 chapters.
